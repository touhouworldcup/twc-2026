## Producing an Unofficial Restream
We actively encourage the community to produce unofficial regional restreams in different languages! There are two ways to do this in accordance with Section 5.2 "Usage Guidelines for unofficial Restreams" of the [TWC Handbook](https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vQrmgcwhgKARoUnk5BPE0Oyv4fAgHilZs1pUa1RQJtm0X_z93L8eI0lNt1Y-iZQK3v4_Ab9vx1HzpkN/pub#h.scgkjrx4iyzp).

**Capturing the World Feed:** You are completely free to capture the raw video of the World Feed directly from [twitch.tv/touhouworldcup](https://twitch.tv/touhouworldcup) and add your own commentary over it. You do not need to use this code, set up NodeCG, or get a sync key to do this. Just make sure you are following the restream guidelines in the Handbook.

**Integrated Regional Stream:** If you want to use this broadcast bundle to generate the official localized overlays for your language (like the official EN, JP, CN, and ES streams do), you must be approved. Reach out to us at [contact@touhouworldcup.com](mailto:contact@touhouworldcup.com) and we will follow up with further information. You do not have to become a permanent official stream to do this, although we would always be happy to welcome new regions to TWC!
